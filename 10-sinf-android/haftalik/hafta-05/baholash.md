# 10-sinf (Advanced Android): 5-hafta baholash qaydnomasi

**Mavzular:**
1. build.gradle faylining asosiy tuzilmasi va modullararo bog'lanishi: Gradle, `settings.gradle`, project va module-level fayllar, `defaultConfig`, `include`, `project(...)`
2. Build variantlar (debug, release) va ularning farqi: `buildTypes`, R8, `buildConfigField`, `productFlavors`
3. Gradle yordamida kutubxonalarni ulash va versiyalarni boshqarish: `dependencies`, versiya turlari, `force`, `exclude`, version catalog
